# Batch 01: scripts for review

Both brands belong to **Elevate Vision Digital**. Each brand's content posts to its own accounts: **@buildwithdb** and **albertaweb.design**. End cards show that brand's handle and URL.

Format: 9:16, 25–40 s, your cloned voice, kinetic captions, drone B-roll.
Each paragraph is one beat (one shot). `*word*` marks an emphasis word (gradient glitch).
B-roll hints are MediaForge search queries; the pipeline picks the best matching drone clip per beat.
**Type:** AD has a direct CTA and can be boosted or run as a paid ad. ORG is organic, built for reach and follows, with a soft CTA.

> Before posting: realistic AI voice should be labelled as AI-generated on TikTok and Instagram (both have a toggle).
