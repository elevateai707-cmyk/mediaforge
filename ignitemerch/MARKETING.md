Bootstrapped digital kits, $29–$149. Buyer imports a file tonight (Shopify/Etsy ops, small agencies, faceless channels). Skip students, prompt collectors, white-label resellers.

**Motion:** product-led checkout + marketing-led demand. No sales calls at this ACV. **Do not buy ads until three organic creatives have sold.** The launch engine is the kits themselves (UGC Floor, Faceless Shorts Desk, Outbound Sequence Press).

North star: **paid orders per week**, not sessions.

## 90 days

**Weeks 1–2 — Unblock.** Domain, Search Console, Stripe live, real access email. Three Heat Press proof clips. Forty outbound emails. Five UGC hooks from the CSV.

**Weeks 3–4 — Floor.** Two shorts/week. Two SEO pages. One Discord/forum thread that is actually useful. First reviews only if they exist.

**Weeks 5–8 — Velocity.** Double the channel that produced the first order. If none, tighten the Heat Press clip (failed thing → file → price), do not add Instagram Reels as a fourth network.

**Weeks 9–12 — Compound.** Push the $149 bundle to buyers of lot 01. Membership only after a first purchase. Affiliate outreach with a zip sample, not a deck.
