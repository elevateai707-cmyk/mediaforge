# Design

Heat-press catalog, not a cyber dashboard.

- Scene: a midnight print buyer under a tungsten lamp.
- Color: committed ember on warm soot (OKLCH). Bone paper for tickets.
- Type: Big Shoulders Display, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Azeret Mono.
- Layout: numbered lots, lookbook rows, sharp corners, perforation on the cart ticket.
- Motion: 0.96 press, expo ease, staggered rise. No layout animation.
