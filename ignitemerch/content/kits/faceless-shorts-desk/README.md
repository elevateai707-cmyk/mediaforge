# 30-Day Faceless Shorts Desk
Version 1.0.0 · Commercial license

A month of faceless short-form for one niche: selling a digital kit. Scripts, B-roll lists, and a posting cadence. No face required.

## Why this SKU
Creator prompt packs sell when they are a desk (calendar + scripts + shots), not 500 untitled prompts.

## You get
1. `calendar/30-days.csv` — day, hook, promise, CTA, platform.
2. `scripts/` — 30 scripts at 18–28 seconds.
3. `broll.md` — screen recordings you already have.
4. `thumbnails.md` — 8 title patterns, no fake view counts on the thumbnail.
5. `repurpose.md` — Shorts to X to email, one pass.

## Cadence
- 5 posts a week. Skip Saturday. Sunday is a recap of one proof.
- Do not post the same hook twice in 10 days.
