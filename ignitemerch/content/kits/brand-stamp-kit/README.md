# Brand Stamp Kit
Version 1.0.0 · Commercial license

A small identity press for a digital-product brand: mark, type, color, and cover recipes. Built to sit next to Heat Press Photo Desk.

## Why this SKU
Generic Midjourney packs are crowded. A locked brand system that survives 20 SKUs is what shops actually need.

## You get
1. `mark.md` — construction of a stamp mark (no gradient wordmarks).
2. `type.md` — one display, one body, one mono. Pairing rules.
3. `color.md` — OKLCH tokens and when ember is allowed to drench.
4. `covers.md` — 6 social cover layouts as type-only posters.
5. `workflows/stamp-mark.json` — ComfyUI graph for stamp textures, not logos from noise.

## Ban list
Inter, purple haze, glass cards, fake 4.9 stars.
