# Marketplace QA

Reject a still if any box fails. Do not upload "close enough."

## Amazon
- [ ] 1:1 or 3:4, longest side ≥ 1600px
- [ ] Pure white or cyclorama recipe only for MAIN
- [ ] Product ≥ 85% of frame
- [ ] No badge, no text, no watermark
- [ ] Logo readable at 200px thumbnail

## Shopify
- [ ] 1:1 plus 4:5 lifestyle
- [ ] Filename `sku-linen.jpg` not `IMG_4033.jpg`
- [ ] Alt text names the object, not "AI product photo"
- [ ] Lifestyle does not invent a second SKU

## Etsy
- [ ] First image is the honest product
- [ ] Lifestyle comes after
- [ ] No fake "bestseller" badge in the frame
- [ ] Color in photo matches the listing variation

## Universal kill
- Melted type
- Extra fingers or extra bottles
- Background seam
- Product floating with no contact shadow (lifestyle only)
