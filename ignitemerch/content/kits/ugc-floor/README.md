# UGC Floor: 80 Hooks + Shot Lists
Version 1.0.0 · Commercial license

A working floor for TikTok and Meta UGC ads that sell digital kits. Not a pile of generic “viral hooks.”

## Why this SKU
Prompt packs that name a job beat generic ChatGPT bundles. This one is for people running paid or organic UGC for a digital product with a $29–$79 price.

## You get
1. `hooks/80-openers.csv` — hook, platform, product price band, and the proof line that follows.
2. `shots/ugc-15s.md` — 15-second shot list with avatar on-screen cap at 30%.
3. `shots/ugc-30s.md` — 30-second version with B-roll beats.
4. `scripts/meta-primary-text.md` — primary text blocks that do not repeat the hook.
5. `compliance.md` — synthetic media and “paid partnership” lines.

## 30% screen-time rule
Avatar or talking head occupies at most 30% of runtime. The rest is product, screen capture, or B-roll. If the face stays on screen past that, cut.

## Sample hook pattern
1. Open on the failed thing (“I paid $180 for product photos”).
2. Smash cut to the desk (this kit, this workflow).
3. Show one real output.
4. Price and what they get tonight.
5. CTA that names the file they will download.
