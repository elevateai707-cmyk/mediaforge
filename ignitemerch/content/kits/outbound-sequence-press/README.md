# Outbound Sequence Press
Version 1.0.0 · Commercial license

A 4-touch outbound desk for selling a $47–$79 digital kit to a named ICP. Email plus LinkedIn. No fake personalization tokens.

## Why this SKU
Cold sequences still sell when they are specific. This one assumes you sell operator kits, not coaching.

## You get
1. `sequences/email-4.md` — four emails, 70–110 words each.
2. `sequences/linkedin-4.md` — connect, bump, proof, ask.
3. `icp.md` — who to skip (students, “prompt engineers,” agencies that want white-label).
4. `proof-lines.md` — how to cite a real artifact without inventing metrics.
5. `stop.md` — when to stop (two no’s, or silence after touch 4).

## Tone
Plain. No “quick question.” No “I noticed your amazing brand.” Name the job and the file.
